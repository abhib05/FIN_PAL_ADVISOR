Ten calculators, written in Python

Every number FinPal says out loud is produced by a deterministic function in the backend. The language model decides what to ask you — it never does the arithmetic.

🏠

Hostel, PG and rent

Separates what your family pays directly from what leaves your own hands, so the budget reflects reality.

💳

BNPL trap check

Counts your pay-later apps, the monthly total and any missed payments, then scores how close you are to trouble.

🛟

Emergency fund target

Sizes a realistic cushion against your essential spend — 3, 6 or 12 months depending on how steady your income is.

📉

Debt payoff order

Ranks education loans, card balances and family borrowing by interest rate, so repayments go where they cost most.

📈

SIP projection

Compounds a monthly SIP to graduation month by month, at rates you can question, with the assumptions stated.

📱

Subscription audit

Adds up the small recurring charges — streaming, storage, gym — and shows the annual figure nobody quotes you.

🧮

EMI and instrument comparison

Works out EMIs and puts FDs, RDs, index funds and PPF side by side in plain rupees.

🎯

FI number and priorities

Answers “what should I do first?” with an ordered list — cushion, high-interest debt, then investing.
